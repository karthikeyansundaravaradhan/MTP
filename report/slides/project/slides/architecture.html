One frozen encoder, three ways to use it

11

The whole system rests on one frozen DINOv2 ViT-S/14, run on 112 pixel IR face crops at 2 frames per second. Nothing in the encoder is trained. Top row: V-star averages the tokens of a 60 second window and applies one linear layer, 3,843 parameters. Calibrated V-star subtracts the driver’s reference token from their first four alert minutes. Middle row: 27 interpretable event features from the 60 fps landmarks, fused by averaging log-probabilities. Bottom row: VIGIL, a mixture of the vision expert and a telemetry plus clock expert, mixed by a gate that only looks at vision quality.
