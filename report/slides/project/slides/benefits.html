Benefits and applications

Cheap, contact-free and safe when sensors fail

Benefits

Contact-free: one IR camera, no wearables; works in the dark

New drivers, no labels: 0.446 / LOSO 0.471 out of the box; 4 unlabelled alert minutes lift it to 0.536

Tiny and fast: 3,843-parameter head, 5–80 KB models, 712 frames/s on a laptop

Fails safe: VIGIL hands over to telemetry + clock when the camera drops out

Explainable: 27 blink, mouth and posture features; honest, reproducible benchmark

Applications

In-car driver monitoring for drowsiness warnings in new vehicles

Fleets: trucks, buses and ride-hailing; fatigue alerts and shift planning

Operators: mining, heavy machinery and rail

Research: simulator studies and a UL-DD benchmark

21

What this buys in practice. The system needs only one infrared camera, so it works at night and without wearables. It works on new drivers with no labels from them, and four minutes of normal driving at the start make it better. The trained parts are tiny and the encoder runs at hundreds of frames per second on a laptop, so it fits embedded hardware. If the camera fails, VIGIL hands over to telemetry and time instead of producing nonsense. The event features keep decisions explainable. The natural users are in-car driver monitoring, commercial fleets, operators of heavy machinery and rail, and researchers who need an honest benchmark on UL-DD.
