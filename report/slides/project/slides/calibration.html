Calibration subtracts each driver’s reference appearance, taken from the first four minutes of their alert drive, with no labels. It is the largest single gain, plus 0.075, and holds under LOSO. The 27 event features are interpretable and alone match the foundation model at 0.480. Fused, the two give 0.536 and 0.525. The calibrated models are the only ones that beat the clock significantly, and their predictions correlate with KSS at 0.63 after removing time in drive.
