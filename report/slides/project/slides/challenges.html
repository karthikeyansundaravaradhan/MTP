Six challenges shaped every design decision. Drivers differ enormously: the same model scores from 0.03 to 0.99 depending on the driver. There are only 290 labels, so anything large overfits. The labels are self-reports with a fuzzy middle class, and Alert is rare in the drowsy drive. Two confounds sit in the data: sleepiness rises with time in every drive, and the two drives differ in lighting and clothing, which colour cameras pick up. Finally, the face is small in the IR frame, cameras fail, and everything had to run on one laptop.
