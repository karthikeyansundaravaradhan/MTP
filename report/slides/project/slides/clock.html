Two controls frame everything that follows. Sleepiness rises within every drive, so a logistic regression on time-in-drive already reaches 0.366. And because the Drowsy and Alert drives differ, time plus the drive label reaches 0.70. Any vision model that can tell the two recordings apart, through lighting or clothing, can score high without seeing drowsiness. We test for both.
