The full comparison on one table, all under the same subject-independent protocol. The clock, the sequence models and the end-to-end CNN all sit between 0.26 and 0.37. Frozen DINOv2 features reach 0.446, and 0.471 under LOSO. Events alone reach 0.48 but lose more under LOSO. The calibrated models are best on every metric: 0.536 pooled, 0.525 LOSO, epoch F1 0.545, ordinal agreement 0.52, and they are the only ones significantly above the clock. For context, the only other subject-independent UL-DD result is a tabular model at 0.362 macro-F1, and the dataset paper’s 84 to 88 percent uses stratified folds that mix drivers.
