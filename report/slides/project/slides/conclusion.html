Conclusion

Honest, robust, and clear about what it measures

Honest bar: built up from floors, every conventional baseline ties the clock, 0.366

Better features: frozen DINOv2 + 3,843 parameters reach 0.446 / LOSO 0.471

Calibration: 4 unlabelled minutes + events give 0.536 / 0.525, beating the clock

Robustness: VIGIL keeps 0.349 with the camera off, against 0.186

The caveat: on UL-DD, the gains over the clock are drive-level; within a drive, time still wins.

Next: several drives per state per driver, denser labels, and models optimised for within-drive ordering.

To close. Built up from the simplest baselines, the honest bar is the clock: hand-built features and an end-to-end CNN do not clear it. Frozen foundation features clear it with a tiny head. Four unlabelled alert minutes and interpretable events push to 0.54, the only models that beat the clock significantly. VIGIL keeps working when the camera fails. And the caveat that answers the title: on UL-DD, what separates these models from the clock is mostly which drive they are watching. To go further we need datasets with several drives per state per driver and denser labels. Thank you, happy to take questions.
