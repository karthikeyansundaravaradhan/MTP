The confusion matrices show the shape of the errors. The clock and the end-to-end CNN cannot recognise Alert. V-star spreads errors everywhere. The calibrated models almost never confuse Alert with Drowsy, which is why ordinal agreement jumps, but they call about half of the Drowsy windows Low-vigilant: the adjacent boundary remains hard, as in the UTA-RLDD literature.
