Three things to remember about UL-DD. First, the label budget is tiny: 290 epochs; the 4,532 windows are overlapping views of them. Second, KSS rises inside every drive, so time-on-task is a confound. Third, two thirds of KSS variance is between the Alert and Drowsy drive, so a model that recognises the recording scores well. Every result is subject-independent: 5-fold over contiguous driver letters, and LOSO.
