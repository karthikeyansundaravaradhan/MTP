Gradio demo

Replay drives, simulate blackouts, watch VIGIL’s gate, upload video

22

Everything ran on a MacBook with an M4 and 16 GB. The final models are tens of kilobytes on top of the public DINOv2 weights. The inference module scores a UL-DD drive with all five models in about two seconds, or an uploaded IR video through a Haar face detector. The Gradio demo replays any drive with honest out-of-fold predictions, like this out-of-fold view of driver K’s two drives, shows the IR frame and model input, and lets you black out the camera to watch VIGIL’s gate open.
