Four hypotheses were fixed before the final evaluation and tested with exact subject-level permutation tests and Holm correction. None is significant: with 16 drivers and per-driver F1 ranging from 0.03 to 0.99, the test has little power below about 0.15 F1. We report effect sizes and intervals instead of claiming significance, and we are explicit about what the results do not show.
