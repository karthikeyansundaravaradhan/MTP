4

The literature splits cleanly in two. Models evaluated with the same people in train and test report 95 to 99 percent. Under subject-wise evaluation on naturalistic KSS, the best results are 52 to 65 percent, and fine-tuning a large video transformer overfits. Massoz showed that 60 second windows matter, which is why we use them. On UL-DD itself, the dataset paper benchmarks only features with stratified folds, and the one subject-independent study reaches macro-F1 0.36, with a time-only model at 0.30. From fusion work we took modality dropout and quality-aware gating, and from foundation-model work the case for frozen features with a linear head. Three gaps remain, and they become our contributions.
