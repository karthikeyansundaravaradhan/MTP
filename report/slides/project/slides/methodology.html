Methodology

One pipeline and one protocol for every model

1 · Windows

60 s, 15 s stride, on the 60 fps video clock

2 · Face crop

IR, landmark box, 112 px, 2 fps

3 · Frozen encoder

DINOv2 ViT-S/14, 768-d token per frame

4 · Small heads

mean-pool + linear; driver calibration; 60 fps events

5 · Fusion

log-prob averaging; VIGIL gated fallback

Splits

5-fold by contiguous driver letters and LOSO over 16 drivers; no driver in both train and test

Model selection

Inner 4-fold subject-wise CV inside training drivers; never the test drivers; 3 seeds averaged

Metrics

Pooled macro-F1 (Alert is scarce) with 95% driver-bootstrap CI; epoch F1, QWK, AUC

Controls

Clock baseline, session probe, 0–60 s blackouts; exact permutation tests, Holm-corrected H1–H4

Calibration protocol: only the first 240 s of the driver’s Alert drive, unlabelled, is available at test time.

The method in one picture. Every model reads the same 60 second windows, anchored on the video clock. Vision models take a 112 pixel IR face crop at two frames per second, pass it through a frozen DINOv2 encoder, and train only a small head; calibration and event features are added on top, and fusion is either fixed log-probability averaging or VIGIL’s gated fallback. The protocol is what makes the numbers honest: drivers are never shared between train and test, all model selection happens inside the training drivers, the headline metric is pooled macro-F1 with confidence intervals over drivers, and every model is compared against a clock and a session-identity probe, with pre-registered, Holm-corrected tests.
