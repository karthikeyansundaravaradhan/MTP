The measurement problem

Most drowsiness accuracies measure the wrong thing

95–99.6%

Random frame or clip splits, same people in train and test; mostly acted, binary NTHU-DDD

52–65%

Three-class KSS with held-out drivers, naturalistic drowsiness (UTA-RLDD)

< 60%

Human judges watching the same videos, same three-class task

On UL-DD, the dataset paper reports 83.8–88.0% with stratified folds. Re-implemented with drivers held out, the same model drops to 30–34%.

Hassan 2025; Ghoddoosian 2019; Wolter 2025; Bodaghi 2025; ildahun 2025

3

The field’s headline numbers come from splits that let the same person appear in train and test. Under subject-independent, naturalistic KSS labels, published results are 52–65% three-class accuracy, and humans do under 60%. UL-DD’s own paper is in the leaky group: an independent re-implementation fell from 75% on a random split to about 30% per driver. So the question for this thesis is what an honest number looks like on UL-DD video.
