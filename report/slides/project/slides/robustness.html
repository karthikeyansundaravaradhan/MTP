C3 · VIGIL (M8)

When the camera fails, VIGIL falls back instead of collapsing

Clean input: 0.428 vs 0.430, no harm (p = 0.80)

Camera off: 0.349 vs 0.186 (+0.163, p = 0.076)

Gate opens 0.13 → 0.93; the residual design’s gate never moved

15

VIGIL is trained with simulated camera blackouts of 15 to 60 seconds, so the gate sees vision fail. On clean input it equals the vision model. With the camera fully off for a window, the vision model collapses to 0.186, predicting one class, while VIGIL keeps 0.349, close to the clock, because the gate opens to 0.93 and hands the decision to the telemetry plus clock expert. An earlier residual-gate design looked robust too, but its gate never moved: that negative result is why VIGIL is a mixture of experts. The effect is large but not significant after Holm with 16 drivers.
